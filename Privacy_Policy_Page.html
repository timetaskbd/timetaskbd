At Time Task BD, we recognize that privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our task management platform. We strive to be transparent about our data practices and ensure you remain in control of your personal information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, complete profile settings, or interact with our platform.

badge

Identity Verification Data

We securely collect Birth Registration Numbers, Date of Birth, and front/back images of National IDs (NID) to verify user identity and ensure platform integrity.

payments

Financial Information

To facilitate payouts, we collect payment details for bKash, Nagad, or Rocket. We also collect and encrypt a 6-digit Withdrawal Security PIN for payout authorization.

social_distance

Social Media Data

We may collect profile information, email, and verification status when you link social media accounts for authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • check_circle Identity verification and account compliance.
  • check_circle Verifying task completion and distributing rewards.
  • check_circle Processing secure financial transactions through local mobile financial services using your encrypted Security PIN.
  • check_circle Improving platform security and preventing fraudulent activity.
  • check_circle Sending critical updates regarding your account or policy changes.

3. Information Sharing

"We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes."

We only share information with third-party vendors and service providers who perform services for us, such as payment processors (bKash/Nagad APIs) and hosting providers. These partners are legally bound to protect your data.

4. Data Security

Time Task BD employs industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information. This includes high-level AES-256 encryption for sensitive data, secure socket layer (SSL) technology, and restricted internal access protocols for stored identification documents (such as NID images).
