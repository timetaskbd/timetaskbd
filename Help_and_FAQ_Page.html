Frequently Asked Questions

How do I withdraw my earnings?

expand_more

To withdraw your earnings, navigate to your Wallet dashboard. Click on the "Withdraw" button, select your preferred payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Crypto, or Digital Wallet), enter the amount, and confirm with your secure PIN. Processing usually takes 24-48 hours depending on your account level.

What is the daily task submission limit?

expand_more

Standard users have a daily limit of 50 tasks. However, this limit can be increased by improving your account accuracy score and maintaining a consistent activity record over 30 days. High-tier accounts enjoy unlimited task submissions.

How to place a bulk order for campaigns?

expand_more

Buyers can place bulk orders by visiting the "Campaign Manager" and selecting "Create New Campaign." Choose the "Bulk Upload" option to import your task list via a CSV or Excel file. Our system will automatically validate the tasks and provide a total cost estimate before you launch.

I forgot my security PIN, what should I do?

expand_more

For your security, PIN resets require email verification. Go to Account Settings > Security > Reset PIN. You will receive a reset link via your registered email address. If you no longer have access to your email, please contact our support team with your ID verification documents.
