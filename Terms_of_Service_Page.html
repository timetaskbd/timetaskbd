assignment 2. Seller (User) Obligations

Account Validity

Sellers must verify their account using government-issued identification. You are responsible for maintaining a single active account. Duplicate accounts created to circumvent platform rules will be merged or banned.

Sheet Submission

All task sheets must be submitted within 24 hours of completion. Data must be honest, verified, and follow the specific formatting guidelines provided in the Seller Dashboard.

Daily Submission Limit

To ensure quality and platform stability, Sellers are restricted to a maximum of 100 account submissions per platform per day. Exceeding this limit may result in temporary submission locks.

credit_card 4. Payment Terms

All financial transactions on Time Task BD are handled through our secure escrow system. Funds are released to Sellers only upon successful verification of the submitted work by the Buyer or our automated audit system. We charge a flat platform fee of 5% on all transactions to maintain service quality.

verified_user 5. Account Verification

To ensure the safety and integrity of our marketplace, Time Task BD implements a rigorous Identity Verification process. Users must provide valid government-issued documentation, specifically Birth Registration or a National ID (NID) card.

In addition to identity documentation, all users are required to set up a 6-digit Withdrawal Security PIN. This PIN is mandatory for all fund transfers and serves as a critical secondary layer of protection for your earnings. Verification status is reviewed annually to ensure continued compliance with international AML (Anti-Money Laundering) standards.

group_add 8. Referral Program

Our Referral Program allows users to earn bonuses by inviting new participants to the platform. For every successful task completed by a referred user, the original referrer will receive a 5% commission based on the task value.

Anti-Fraud Policy

We strictly prohibit fraudulent referral practices. This includes, but is not limited to, creating duplicate accounts to refer yourself, using automated systems to generate sign-ups, or providing misleading information to potential referees. Any user found engaging in these practices will have their referral bonuses voided and their account permanently banned.
